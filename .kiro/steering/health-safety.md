# Health safety rules
- Public or synthetic data only. Never store user health data.
- No diagnosis or treatment claims. Show "Not medical advice" on every page.
- Show ranges (low to high confidence limits), never a single ranked list.
- Cancer is shown in its own view so it does not swamp other conditions.
- Every number links to its source (IHME GBD, CDC PLACES, ClinicalTrials.gov, Census).
- Call it an index, never a prediction.
